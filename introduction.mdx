**iSibusiso Digital Experiences** is a South African digital agency founded and run by Sibusiso Khanye, based in Palmridge, Alberton. The agency exists for one reason: to make high-quality digital solutions accessible to the startups and small businesses that need them most. From a clean, conversion-focused website to a fully featured e-commerce store or a bespoke web application, iSibusiso brings the expertise, tools, and hands-on care that turn ambitious ideas into real online success.

## Our Mission

The South African startup and SMB landscape is full of talented entrepreneurs who deserve a professional online presence but are often underserved by agencies that cater only to large corporates. iSibusiso bridges that gap — delivering agency-grade quality at prices and with a level of personal attention that works for growing businesses. Every project, regardless of size, receives the same focus on strategy, design, and performance.

## Who We Serve

You are the ideal iSibusiso client if you are:

- A **startup** launching a digital product or service for the first time.
- A **small or medium business** that needs a stronger, more professional online presence.
- An **entrepreneur** who wants a custom web application to streamline or automate part of your business.
- An **existing business** that needs better search visibility, faster growth, or a redesigned user experience.
