## Who It's For

Custom web applications are the right solution for:

- **Business management systems** that need to centralise operations, track resources, or automate reporting
- **Customer portals** giving clients or partners secure, self-service access to their data and documents
- **Booking systems** managing appointments, reservations, or service scheduling with real-time availability
- **Dashboards** visualising business metrics, sales data, or operational KPIs in one place

## Common Questions

<Accordion title="What information do I need to provide for a quote?">
  The more context you share, the more accurate your quote will be. Useful information includes: a description of the problem you are solving, the key features you need, any third-party systems the application must connect to, the number of user types or roles, and your preferred timeline. You do not need to have everything figured out — the discovery session is designed to help clarify requirements together.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How long does a custom application take to build?">
  Timelines vary depending on the complexity of the application and the number of features involved. A focused tool with a narrow scope can be delivered relatively quickly, while a multi-role platform with extensive integrations will take longer. Your timeline will be discussed and agreed upon during the discovery and strategy phase before development begins.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I add features to the application after launch?">
  Yes. Every custom application iSibusiso Digital Experiences builds uses a scalable architecture specifically to support future growth. Whether you need new modules, additional user roles, extra integrations, or expanded functionality down the line, the codebase is structured to accommodate those changes without requiring a full rebuild.
</Accordion>
