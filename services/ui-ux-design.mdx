## Why Design Comes First

Skipping the design phase is one of the most expensive mistakes a startup can make. Fixing layout, navigation, or branding issues after development is complete costs significantly more in time and money than getting it right upfront. By investing in UI/UX design before development begins, you reduce rework, align your team around a shared vision, and arrive at launch day with a product your users will actually enjoy using.

## What the Design Process Looks Like

<Steps>
  <Step title="Discovery & Strategy">
    Share your brand goals, target audience, and any existing visual assets. iSibusiso Digital establishes the design direction and defines the key screens and flows to prototype.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Design & Prototype">
    High-fidelity designs are created for each key screen and assembled into an interactive prototype. You click through the experience and provide feedback before anything is built.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Refinement">
    Based on your feedback, designs are refined until you're fully satisfied. Your approval is required before the project moves to development.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Handoff">
    You receive a complete design system and all assets, ready for development. If iSibusiso Digital is also building your product, the handoff is seamless.
  </Step>
</Steps>
