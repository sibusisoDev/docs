## Common Use Cases

Custom web applications are the right choice when your business needs go beyond what a standard website or template platform can offer:

<Accordion title="Business Management Systems">
  Replace spreadsheets and disconnected tools with a single, centralised system that manages your operations, tracks key metrics, and keeps your team aligned.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Customer Portals">
  Give your clients a secure, branded portal where they can view their account, track orders or projects, submit requests, and access documents — all in one place.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Booking & Scheduling Systems">
  Let customers book appointments, services, or resources online with real-time availability, automated confirmations, and calendar integration.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Dashboards & Reporting Tools">
  Visualise your business data in real time with custom dashboards that surface the metrics that matter most to your decision-making.
</Accordion>

## How It Works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Discovery & Strategy">
    You walk iSibusiso Digital through your workflows, pain points, and goals. Together, you define the scope, features, and architecture of your application.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Design & Prototype">
