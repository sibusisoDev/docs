If you're considering working with iSibusiso Digital and have questions before taking the next step, you'll find answers to the most common ones below. Topics range from pricing and timelines to what you need to provide and what happens after your project launches. If your question isn't covered here, don't hesitate to reach out directly — the team is happy to help.

## Common Questions

<Accordion title="How much does a website cost?">
  Website development at iSibusiso Digital starts from **R4,000**. E-commerce stores and custom web applications are scoped and quoted individually, as the cost depends on the features, complexity, and scale of your project. Submit an enquiry to receive a tailored proposal.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How long does a project take?">
  Timelines vary based on the complexity and scope of your project. A standard business website typically takes **2–4 weeks** from approved design to launch. E-commerce stores and custom applications take longer, depending on the number of features and integrations required. Your project timeline will be clearly outlined in your proposal.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Do you work with clients across South Africa?">
  Yes. iSibusiso Digital works remotely with clients anywhere in South Africa. The entire process — from discovery and design review to launch and handover — is handled online, so your location is never a barrier.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What do I need to provide to get started?">
  To get started, it helps to have the following ready:

  - Your **business name** and a brief description of what you do
  - Your **goals** for the project — what you want the website or app to achieve
  - Your **target audience** — who you're trying to reach
  - Any **existing branding** — logos, colour palettes, brand guidelines
  - Your **content** — the text and images you want to appear on the site

  Don't worry if not everything is finalised yet. The discovery call is the right place to work through the details together.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Do you offer ongoing support after launch?">
  Yes. Ongoing support and digital growth optimisation are available after your project goes live. The best time to discuss your post-launch support needs is during the discovery call, so the right arrangement can be built into your proposal from the start.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What technologies do you use?">
  iSibusiso Digital builds with modern, industry-proven technologies — **React** for the frontend, **Node.js** for the backend, and **Tailwind CSS** for styling. Every site and application is built to be fast, fully responsive across all devices, and ready to scale as your business grows.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I see examples of your work?">
  Absolutely. Visit [isibusiso-digital.co.za](https://isibusiso-digital.co.za) to explore the agency's own website — a live demonstration of the design quality, performance, and attention to detail that goes into every project.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I pay?">
  Payment terms are discussed and agreed upon during the proposal stage, before any work begins. The team will walk you through the payment structure as part of your project proposal so there are no surprises.
</Accordion>
