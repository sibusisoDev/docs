Working with iSibusiso Digital is a structured, collaborative experience designed to take you from idea to live product with as little friction as possible. From the moment you submit your enquiry, you're guided through a clear four-stage process — with open communication, regular checkpoints, and full transparency at every step. Here's exactly what that journey looks like.

## Your Client Journey

<Steps>
  <Step title="Initial Enquiry">
    Submit your project details through the contact form at [isibusiso-digital.co.za/#contact](https://isibusiso-digital.co.za/#contact). The team reviews every enquiry and responds **within 24 hours** to acknowledge your submission and outline the next step.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Discovery Call">
    Once your enquiry is received, you'll be invited to a discovery conversation to dig into your goals, target audience, technical requirements, and any constraints. This is your opportunity to ask questions and ensure the team fully understands your vision before any work begins.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Proposal & Quote">
    Based on the discovery session, iSibusiso prepares a scoped proposal that covers the full project breakdown, recommended approach, timeline, and pricing. You review it, ask questions, and approve before anything moves forward.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Design Phase">
    The team creates wireframes and an interactive prototype of your project. You review the designs, provide feedback, and sign off before a single line of production code is written — keeping revisions fast and cost-effective.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Development">
    With approved designs in hand, development begins. You'll receive regular progress updates and check-ins throughout the build, so you're never left wondering where things stand.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Launch & Handover">
    Your project goes live with a full handover — including guidance on managing your site, understanding what was built, and how to get ongoing support. The relationship doesn't end at launch.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Clear communication during the discovery phase leads to faster delivery and fewer revisions. Come prepared with your goals, audience insights, and any reference materials — it makes every subsequent stage smoother.
</Tip>

## The Four Core Stages

Every iSibusiso Digital project follows the same proven four-stage methodology, regardless of scope or budget.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="01 — Discovery & Strategy" icon="magnifying-glass">
    Define the problem, the audience, and the goals. Strategy first — so design and development always serve a clear purpose.
  </Card>
  <Card title="02 — Design & Prototype" icon="pen-ruler">
    Create wireframes and an interactive prototype you can see and feel before development starts. Feedback at this stage is fast and free.
  </Card>
  <Card title="03 — Development" icon="code">
    Build the product iteratively with regular check-ins. You stay informed and involved throughout the entire build phase.
  </Card>
  <Card title="04 — Launch & Growth" icon="rocket">
    Go live with confidence. Post-launch, the team remains available for support, optimisation, and ongoing digital growth.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
