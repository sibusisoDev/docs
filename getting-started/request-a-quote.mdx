The fastest way to kick off your project is by submitting an enquiry through the iSibusiso Digital contact form. Whether you need a business website, an e-commerce store, or a custom web application, providing clear project details upfront means you get a more accurate quote — and a faster turnaround from the team.

## How to Request a Quote

<Steps>
  <Step title="Visit the Contact Form">
    Head to the iSibusiso Digital contact page at [isibusiso-digital.co.za/#contact](https://isibusiso-digital.co.za/#contact) to find the project enquiry form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Your Name and Email Address">
    Fill in your full name and a valid email address so the team can follow up with your personalised quote and proposal.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Your Project Type">
    Choose the type of project you have in mind — for example, **Business Website**, **E-commerce Store**, **Custom Web Portal**, or **UI/UX Design**. This helps the team route your enquiry to the right expertise.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe Your Project in Detail">
    Use the message field to share as much context as possible — your goals, key features, target audience, and any design preferences. The more specific you are, the more accurate and relevant your quote will be.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit and Await a Response">
    Hit **Send** and you're done. The iSibusiso team will review your enquiry and get back to you **within 24 hours** with next steps.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Tips for a Better Quote

Use these tips to make your enquiry as effective as possible, so the proposal you receive is tailored precisely to your needs.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Be Specific" icon="pencil">
    Describe your goals, the features you need, and who your target audience is. Vague briefs lead to vague quotes — detail leads to precision.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Include a Timeline" icon="calendar">
    If you have a launch deadline or a specific date in mind, mention it upfront so the team can confirm feasibility and plan accordingly.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Share Examples" icon="link">
    Link to websites you admire or products with a look-and-feel you're going for. Visual references save time and align expectations early.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Mention Your Budget" icon="coins">
    Sharing a rough budget range — even a ballpark — helps the team tailor the proposal to what's achievable and recommend the right approach.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
