## How It Works

From your first conversation with us to the moment your product goes live, we follow a clear, proven four-step process so you always know what to expect.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Discovery & Strategy">
    We start by getting to know your business, your audience, and your goals so that every decision we make is grounded in your real-world context.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Design & Prototype">
    We craft a visual identity and interactive prototype that lets you see and feel your product before a single line of production code is written.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Development">
    Our team builds your solution using modern tools and best practices, ensuring the final product is fast, secure, and ready to scale.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Launch & Growth">

</Steps>
