Every project iSibusiso Digital Experiences takes on — from a single-page website to a fully custom web application — follows the same four-step delivery methodology. This process exists to protect you: it keeps the project on track, ensures your goals stay front and centre, and gives you clear visibility at every stage. No surprises, no scope creep, no disappearing acts — just a structured journey from idea to live product.

<Steps>
  <Step title="01 — Discovery & Strategy">
    Before any design or code work begins, we invest time in truly understanding your business. This stage covers your business model, the audience you are trying to reach, the problem your product solves, your competitors, and the specific goals you want your digital presence to achieve.

    You can expect a focused kick-off conversation — in person, over a call, or via WhatsApp — where we ask the right questions and listen carefully to your answers. The output is a clear project brief that both you and the iSibusiso team agree on before moving forward. Getting this stage right is what separates a website that looks good from one that actually works for your business.
  </Step>
  <Step title="02 — Design & Prototype">
    With strategy confirmed, we move into the creative phase. This is where your brand comes to life visually — colour palette, typography, layout, and the overall look and feel that will represent you online.

    Rather than handing you static mockups that are hard to evaluate, we build interactive prototypes so you can click through your product and experience it the way your users will. This gives you a real opportunity to request changes before development starts, saving time and cost. Your feedback at this stage shapes the final product, so we actively encourage you to be specific and honest about what you do and do not like.
  </Step>
  <Step title="03 — Development">
    Once the design is signed off, we build. iSibusiso uses modern development tools and industry best practices to ensure your product is not only visually faithful to the approved design but also fast to load, secure against common vulnerabilities, and architected to scale as your business grows.

    Throughout development, you receive regular progress updates so you can see the work taking shape. We flag any technical decisions that affect your requirements and keep the agreed timeline visible at all times. If something changes on your side, we discuss the impact openly rather than absorbing scope silently and billing you later.
  </Step>
  <Step title="04 — Launch & Growth">
    Launch day is a milestone, not an ending. We take your product live in a controlled, tested deployment and monitor its initial performance to catch any issues early. After launch, we stay engaged — tracking how the site performs, identifying opportunities to improve conversion rates or search visibility, and ensuring your digital presence continues to serve your business as it evolves.

    Whether you need a Digital Growth & SEO retainer, periodic updates, or simply the confidence that someone knowledgeable is available when you have questions, iSibusiso is here for the long term.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  The more detail you bring to your first conversation, the faster and smoother your project will run. Think about your target audience, any websites you admire, the features you cannot live without, and your ideal launch date — even rough notes help us hit the ground running.
</Tip>
